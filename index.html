Submit your pen

For the best valuation, photograph the whole pen, cap, nib and any barrel/imprint markings.

📸 For the most accurate valuation, include:
1. Full pen capped, beside a ruler
2. Full pen uncapped, with the cap beside it
3. Sharp close-up of the top of the nib
4. Close-up of the nib underside and feed
5. Any nib hallmark: 14K, 18K, 585 or 750
6. Barrel, cap-band and model markings
7. Pen unscrewed to show the filling system
8. Original box, accessories and a writing sample
